## 0. What kind of knowledge this is

A **physics-department survey** of steel-string shop practice: energy path, wood metrology-by-ear, X-brace mode control, live back, scale/intonation/bridge placement. Closest cousins: Somogyi air-pump / stiffness-weight / live-back doctrine; Gore/Nicoletti mode vocabulary (monopole, dipoles, Helmholtz) without Gore’s measured modal-placement stack or falcate system.

Distinct from:

- Gore Packs 1–8 — mid-scale resonance placement + mobility meters  
- Somogyi 01–02 — same metaphors, different teacher  
- Nicoletti EGB — FRF/Chladni as QC practice (Howman shows Chladni but declines process use)

## 8. Harvested workflow spine (Howman dialect)

```text
Hunt QS / split billets; fist-tap S/W + crack screen
  → Choose top species by tension/bass intent (spruce vs WRC)
  → X-brace (+ optional dome) sized for modes more than bulk structure
  → Carve braces to tap target (cube-rule caution); don’t scallop-as-fashion
  → Basswood linings to isolate; voice back for long dipole / live-back intent
  → Place bridge near body center (scale/neck graph); compensate saddle by tuner
  → Thin finish; design back effort to playing posture
```

Agree with other schools on: efficiency/air-pump, S/W wood pick, mode-aware bracing, live back, free-top ≠ finished.  
Diverge on: meters (ear/tap vs FRF/Y), Chladni as process, falcate vs X, Gore mid-scale triad discipline (not stated here).
