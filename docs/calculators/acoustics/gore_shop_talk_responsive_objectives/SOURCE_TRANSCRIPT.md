## Objectives (~10:29–12:13)

Aim: (1) right monopole mobility — not necessarily high; responsiveness/potential loudness; (2) evenness — resonances off scale tones; (3) musicality — high FRF peak density + ET intonation. Playability & structure assumed.

## Mobility measurement (~13:07–20:42)

Coined term; hybrid easy measurement. Need K and M of monopole. Jig: beam, dial/tire gauge on bridge, ~1 kg lead shot → P≈9.81 N; demo **s≈0.15 mm**; K=P/s. Plug soundhole; tap f; M=K/(2πf)²; Y=1/√(KM). Raise Y by lowering K and/or M **in proportion** to hold target f. Classical typical ~20, great ~30s; steel typical 10–12, great low 20s. “Conspiracy against guitarists.”

## Evenness (~20:48–23:46)

T111/112/113 off scale tones. Table: A=110; ±50¢ → 107/113; pick outer columns. Avoid exact octave T111–T112 (not 90/180). Four semitones T112–T113.

## Musicality (~23:51–28:26)

Matthews & Kohut: 20–30 peaks to 5 kHz, irregular vs harmonics. Live back → peaks/tone; non-live → volume. Nut+saddle compensation; frets 1–3 sharp mechanism; nut shortens → drop tension all frets; ~½¢ residual with full method.

## Nut Q (~28:31–33:09)

Nut compensation not cancelled by fretting — global cents. Half-split of saddle-only compensation (e.g. 4 mm → 2+2). Book chapter ~20 pages. 1 mm nut ≈ 3 cents/fret.

## SRC & Q (~33:24–37:48)

SRC ~ √(E/ρ³); choose by type (classical high; stage low). Q: some hear difference; Dresden/Pacific Rim blind tests no significant difference; paper pending; B/R/Y video same FRF different damping.
