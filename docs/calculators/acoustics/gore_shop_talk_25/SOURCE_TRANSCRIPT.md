## Falcate & humidity (~11:05–13:45)

Steve (Sydney): humidity effects of falcate vs other bracing during/after build.

Gore: Build at ~40–45% RH for any guitar. After build, falcate benefits: fewer continuous cross-grain braces than X/lattice; mainly the bridge spans width. Top pumps less with humidity. Extreme RH: secondary falcates bulge edges — bridge looks sunk but isn’t. Action hardly changes — fairly impervious for playability.

## Name & origin (~14:03–18:39)

Falcate = sickle-shaped; named by Michael Timothy (vs boomerang). Origin: sail tape-drive stress tapes (Ole Coleus) + engineer desire to put stiffness where max stress is (critique of X layout).

## T112 too low after close (~21:02–24:32)

Good mid-scale: steel 170/180/190; classical ~190. Rescue: stiff/light bridge (CF laminate, low-density wood, maybe wider); removing top material lowers f further. Way low → replace top. 170 OK on 000; 160 often muddy. Aim a bit high; long mass-load down hurts responsiveness.

## Side mass / coins (~24:33–29:36)

Mold lowered top f; coins on sides proved mass not stiffness; 4DOF model agrees. Classical teacher coins-in-bag → permanent ~300–400 g side mass → much louder (~6–10 dB recalled). Any guitar.

## Retop / back frequency (~31:28–34:03)

Vibrational stiffness = repeatability, not a T112. Live back: T113 four semitones above T112 (190→240). Back-only B11 slightly below finished T113; tune B11 to 240 → ~242–243 with top → trim to 240.

## Laminated sides (~34:15–38:09)

More stiffness/stability/mass; less adjustable than side masses. Flexible walls lower Helmholtz vs rigid cavity. Solid sides can shift air ~2–3 Hz via compliance. Gore: laminate effort usually not worth it.

## Epoxy 105/207 (~38:15–40:26; ~58:36–1:00:16)

207 non-bloom; 3:1; uses for glue/laminate/coat. CF+epoxy standard; longevity known ~40 years; other mixes need lifetime trial.

## Classical 180/226 (~40:33–41:41)

180 lowish for classical; prefer brace height change with book F number; 180 often implies mass loading if top already stiff enough.

## Live-back 45° braces (~41:41–42:24)

Unknown higher-mode effect; suspects suppression so back moves as one.

## Marty radial vs falcate (~42:30–50:04)

Meyer “third resonance” under centerline excitation / no live back = long dipole. Marty discontinuous under bridge → active long dipole / more rotation. Falcate opposite (stiff under bridge, ~2°). Prefer cross-tripole for first-string range.

## Body damping / bevels (~50:35–52:36)

Body against live back dampens, doesn’t kill. Bevel can increase top damping; armrest that holds arm away better.

## Dread cube rule (~52:42–53:47)

Same F numbers OK; scale braces with span³ (and thickness³ relationship).

## Side sound port (~54:34–55:48)

Changes A0 (position+size); side-only needs similar area magnitude; less out-front; couples to top f.

## Spreadsheets (~55:54–58:31)

Unlikely to ship book sheets; write your own; books are a design toolbox.
