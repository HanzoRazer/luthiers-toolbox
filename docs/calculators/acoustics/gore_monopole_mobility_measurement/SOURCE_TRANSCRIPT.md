**~0:00–0:38 — Definition:** Chatter about monopole mobility. Indicator of soundboard responsiveness. Calculation approximating average admittance \(Y\): velocity/sound from string force. Term invented by Trevor Gore — study his teachings.

**~0:52–1:35 — Formula:** \(Y = 1/\sqrt{k m}\); \(k\) stiffness, \(m\) mass. Restated at the bench.

**~1:38–2:12 — Units / force:** Effective mass in kg; force 9.81 N; stiffness N per metre deflection. Apply 1 kg; \(F=ma=1\times9.81=9.81\,\mathrm{N}\).

**~2:14–3:19 — Jig / deflection:** Monopole mobility jig (Dan Langhofer, Carrico). Zero digital caliper; place 1 kg (comes with jig) in cup; deflection **27 mm**. Measure in mm. Alternate: load, zero, unload; average if needed.

**~3:21–3:44 — Solve \(k\):** Stiffness = force / deflection (spoken as 1 kilo / 27 — see annotated unit caveat).

**~3:46–4:07 — Solve \(m\):** \(m = k / (2\pi f)^{2}\) — inversion of \(f=\frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{k/m}\).

**~4:14–4:50 — Uncoupled \(f\):** Loosen strings + yogurt cup in soundhole, or Jeff Nor plug under strings. Isolates uncoupled top from box/back air influence.

**~4:52–6:18 — Guitar Tap:** David Smith (Oregon); free PC, nominal Mac App Store fee; iOS/iPad. Onboard or USB mic. Thumb tap or Hub of Acoustics hammer. Demo uncoupled top **180.7 Hz**.

**~6:43–7:36 — Result / thresholds:** Compute \(Y\). Higher ⇒ more responsive. Steel/acoustic: above ~11–12 starting responsive; classical above ~20. Demo **31.3**; Gore: above ~20 very responsive — “off the charts.”

**~7:38–8:06 — Spreadsheet:** Carrico.com spreadsheet with the rig — enter force, deflection, uncoupled top resonance → mobility number.

**~8:09–8:41 — Upcoming apps:** Luther Academy apps by Trevor Gore and Rick Mallaloy (Seattle area): four degrees of freedom, wood properties, monopole mobility, more.

**~8:44–8:45 — Close:** Happy building.
