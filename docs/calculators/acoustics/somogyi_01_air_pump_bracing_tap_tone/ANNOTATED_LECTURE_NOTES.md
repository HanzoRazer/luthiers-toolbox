### 4.2 Sustain vs quick decay (flamenco / jazz)  
**Timestamps:** ~5:07–6:02  

High-Q tendency (else equal): sustaining activity — fine for many musics. Lower-Q softer/spongier: absorbs energy → bright, quick, rapidly decaying, zingy — good for flamenco and certain jazz where old notes must clear for new ones.
