## What belongs here

Book-match and billeting workflow, factory overbuild vs hand voicing, deflection-jig culture vs tap culture, species/billet storytelling, torrefaction/reclaimed lore, client climate/use interviews, buyer guidance (“play the guitar not the label”), assembly-line vs one-off process.

**Deliverable for Toolbox:** dialect cards, stage-gate UX copy, intake questionnaires, anti-patterns — **not** calculator defaults or physics thresholds.

**Does not belong here as primary:** FRF target triads, mobility formulas, mode-label maps (those → physics lane). A shop pack may *motivate* with physics (“finite energy → light/stiff”) without becoming a physics pack.
