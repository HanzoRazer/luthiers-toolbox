## 7. Iulius Orchestra design notes

### 7.1 Offset soundhole — acoustic + structural  
**Timestamps:** ~17:36–19:06  

Move hole off active board area → more radiating space. Off ~**70 kg** string-tension axis → stability, less bellying. Balance player radiation (not too much toward player).

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Classification | **GN** |
| Link | Gore/Somogyi port/position air levers — related |
| Toolbox destination | Geometry dialect card — do not rewrite Martin defaults |

**Point ID:** N58  

---

### 7.2 13-fret overlapping bolt-on neck; curved upper bout  
**Timestamps:** ~19:06–22:00  

Personal design after Martin/Benedetto period. Overlapping neck, whole single-piece neck wood, no 14-fret ski jump, bolt-on service, lower action possible. Curve into upper bout → stiffness + organic look. Flattop with radiused lower bout + curved upper — **not** archtop.

### 7.3 Active back: denser ebony to limit back mobility  
**Timestamps:** ~22:27–24:06  

Active back for midrange richness. Too-low density → back too mobile → absorbs top energy → lose projection/clarity. Mobility concept applies to **back** too; slightly higher back mass lowers back mobility to target. Corrects “denser = more reflective” oversimplification.
