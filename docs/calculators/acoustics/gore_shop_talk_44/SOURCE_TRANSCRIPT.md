**CF soft bridges (~10:00):** Saddle on softer wood + CF — no tonal problem (walnut/blackwood). Dylla recordings as evidence.

**Back laminated kerfing (~11:30):** Unknown; uses open triangular; solid linings may limit side-flop air tuning.

**Classical CF bridge+patch (~14:42):** I-beam-like; sensible; humidity corrugation help if RH not constant (~45%).

**Rotation vs mobility (~16:54):** Both stiffness proxies; 2° rough max to avoid S-top; mobility is what you hear — prioritize high Y; don’t fetishize rotation.

**Ukulele falcate (~20:26):** Done; same physics (ANZLF / UU).

**Laminated sides + side mass (~22:08):** Stiffness/mass knobs; laminate limits adjustability. Side mass moves T112 node outboard; concentric dipole cancellation reduced → much louder + trims T112; reversible.

**Live back (~26:25):** Sides don’t preclude; live=tone (peaks/T113); non-live=volume.

**Free plate (~28:46):** Poor finished predictor (coupling); identical assemblies impractical.

**CF cloth (~31:12):** ~200 gsm plain weave, 3k tow; AU less fluffy (Toray?).

**Archtop (~34:16):** Speculation — no f-doubled tension drive; heavier tops.

**Dread 2°/165 Hz (~37:40):** Large guitars: 2° max, go stiffer (~half for falcate); T112 prefer 170/180 (160 muddy, 190 shrill).

**Books (~39:06):** Ed3 set; index/pickguard; Vol.3 maybe later.

**Torrefied (~43:55):** Unused; denser↓ stiff↑ strength↓ glue harder.

**Tentellones (~45:08):** Small side-stiffness effect.

**Bracing vs species (~45:44):** Many systems work; overall K+M for modes + Y.

**Mass loading (~48:53):** Right-place mass; T112 node outboard.

**Scallop (~50:22):** Often returns X toward symmetry; falcate symmetric efficient.

**Brace tuck (~53:38):** Encastre ~+25% stiffness; don’t tuck for responsiveness.

**Solid linings (~55:08):** More standing-wave reflection → volume.

**Wolf (~56:30):** Loud/short; match string↔body; mid-scale 50¢.

**Modal tuning (~58:57):** Place mode frequencies via Chladni/modes.

**Better guitar (~1:01:15):** Playability + structure + musicality; responsive harder to intonate.

**Thin panel / conspiracy (~1:05:44):** Thin+CF braces OK; banjo floor; High Street ~50% of potential.
