# Authority boundary — PEM-BUILD-WITNESS-001

A successful capture and a successful verification do two narrow things.

Verification means the package's regular files match the manifest's paths, byte lengths, media types, and SHA-256 digests, and the records follow this contract. The verifier reports that as `INTEGRITY_VERIFIED`.

When the required photographs, environment rows, mass, dimension, protocol, and three repeated trials plus a background capture are present, the verifier may also report `BUILD_RESPONSE_OBSERVED`. That is a statement about the tested build as a coupled system.

## What those results do not authorize

- They do not establish intrinsic wood modulus, damping, or density.
- They do not verify a botanical species. A trade name stays `DECLARED_ONLY` unless an independent evidence file is cited, and even then this increment does not compare that identity with another species.
- They do not compare the build with the unreconciled literature or MB Sound population. That comparison stays `REFERENCE_COMPARISON_BLOCKED` while `PEM-LITERATURE-MB-RECONCILE-002R` is blocked.
- They do not approve a scientific conclusion. The package stays `SCIENTIFIC_INTERPRETATION_PENDING` and `METHOD_REVIEW_PENDING`.
- Missing calibration is recorded as `CALIBRATION_UNVERIFIED`. Capture can still be complete. Scientific promotion cannot.
- They do not approve a design, a production release, a CAM program, or a machine movement. Those fields are rejected. The report's gate lines stay closed.
- They do not estimate a missing measurement or substitute a room condition for a missing temperature or humidity.

## Corrections

Raw bytes are not rewritten. A later manifest replacement may add `derived/` and `reports/` files. It refuses a changed or added file outside those directories. A correction of the build record is a new witness id that names the id it supersedes. An id that was burned for a write that did not finish cannot be reused.

## Private evidence

The real package, including audio and photographs, stays outside this repository until the owner decides otherwise. Synthetic tests generate their own short tones. They are not recordings of an instrument.
