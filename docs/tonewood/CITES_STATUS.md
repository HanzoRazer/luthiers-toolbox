# CITES Status Reference — Lutherie Timbers

> **Checked:** 2026-11-14 against **Species+** (UNEP-WCMC's official database for the CITES Secretariat), read through its API `https://www.speciesplus.net/api/v1/taxon_concepts/{id}.json`. EU status is from the same records (Commission Regulation (EU) 2026/1383, in force 29 June 2026).
> **Use:** this is the single CITES reference for `docs/tonewood/`. Other documents should point here, not restate a status. Re-check before buying or shipping: listings change at every CITES Conference of the Parties (CoP).
> **Not legal advice.** Permits depend on your country and shipment; confirm with your national CITES authority.


## How to read this

- **Appendix I** — commercial trade in wild specimens essentially prohibited.
- **Appendix II** — trade allowed with export permits. What a permit covers depends on the **annotation**. Many timber annotations cover only raw wood, which **includes guitar blanks and sawn sets**, and leave finished goods out.
- **"Not listed"** — no CITES control. The species may still be threatened on the IUCN Red List, which is a conservation assessment, not a trade rule.
- **EU Annex D** — an EU monitoring list. Imports into the EU are recorded, but no CITES permit is required.

| **#17** (Khaya, Afzelia, African Pterocarpus, ipe, Dipteryx, afrormosia, *P. erinaceus*) | "Logs, sawn wood, veneer sheets, plywood and transformed wood" | Not covered, but **blanks and sawn sets are covered** |

| **#10, amended at CoP20** (pernambuco) | All parts and derivatives, except finished instruments, parts and accessories moved for **non-commercial** purposes (performance, personal use, repair…). **Zero quota for wild-harvested specimens traded commercially** | Exempt **only** for non-commercial movement |

## Entandrophragma and Okoume: what actually happened

An EU proposal to list *Entandrophragma* and Okoume in Appendix II was discussed before CoP20 (Samarkand, Nov–Dec 2025). The EU postponed it; ATIBT reports it "remains an objective for CoP21 in 2028" (secondary source). No such proposal appears in the CoP20 proposal list. As of this check the genus is **not CITES-listed** and sits in **EU Annex D** only. Statements that Sapele is "CITES Appendix II" or was "listed at CoP20" are wrong.

## Common errors (all found in this folder before 2026-11-14)

1. "Siamese rosewood — Appendix I." It is **Appendix II**.
2. "Sapele — CITES Appendix II" / "acquire before CoP20." Sapele is **not CITES-listed** (EU Annex D only).
3. "Kiaat / Doussie / Chanfuta / African Padauk — not listed." **All are Appendix II**, under the African *Pterocarpus* and *Afzelia* listings (#17, since 2023-02-23).
4. "Khaya — sustainable / not listed." African *Khaya* is **Appendix II** (#17).
5. "Ipe — CITES-free substitute." Ipe is **Appendix II** (#17, since 2024-11-25).
6. Presenting an IUCN category ("Endangered") as a CITES status.
7. "Finished instruments are exempt" applied to everything. Only #15 (Dalbergia, bubinga) and the narrow non-commercial #10 (pernambuco) exempt instruments. *D. nigra* (App I) has no exemption, and blanks and sawn sets of #17/#6/#5 woods always need permits.
